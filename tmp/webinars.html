Workshops and Cross-training

NCCAT provides access to state-of-the-art instrumentation, including high-end microscopes and direct detectors, as well as specimen preparation equipment, screening microscopes and other necessary ancillary equipment required to solve structures to the highest possible resolution using cryo-electron microscopy methods.  

Our well established cross-training program supports users with diverse background experience and career goals. Our workshops and forums provide opportunities to target specific areas of education and professional development in a larger group format. We also make our educational materials freely available as much a possible, for continuing self-study.
