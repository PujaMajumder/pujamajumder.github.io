Puja Majumder is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Bioscience and Biotechnology at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, where she leads the Majumder Lab. Her research investigates the molecular mechanisms of bacterial antiphage defense, with an emphasis on CRISPR-associated immune effectors and the cellular responses they activate.

She received her MSc in Biotechnology from Jawaharlal Nehru University, graduating First Class First with a Gold Medal, and her PhD in Structural Biology from the Indian Institute of Science under the supervision of Aravind Penmatsa. Her doctoral research examined the structural and functional basis of antibacterial recognition and transport by the multidrug efflux transporter QacA. She subsequently conducted research at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center in the laboratory of Dinshaw J. Patel before joining IIT Kharagpur in June 2026.

Her work has contributed to understanding how CRISPR-associated effectors couple immune signaling to membrane depolarization, nucleotide remodeling, and filament-mediated metabolite depletion. These studies include Cat1, Cad1, and Cam1, reported in Science, Cell, and Nature, respectively. Her laboratory integrates cryo-electron microscopy, X-ray crystallography, biophysics, biochemistry, microbiology, and computational biology to identify new defense systems and establish their functional mechanisms.

Her honors include the Ludwig Cancer Research Postdoctoral Fellowship and Award (2025) and poster awards from the Indian Biophysical Society and an EMBO workshop on membrane protein structural biology.
