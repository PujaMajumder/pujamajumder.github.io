The Majumder Lab at IIT Kharagpur investigates the molecular mechanisms of bacterial antiphage defense. We combine structural biology, biophysics, biochemistry, and microbiology to understand how immune proteins recognize signals and activate a response to infection.

Our work on CRISPR-associated effectors reveals how bacteria open membrane pores, remodel cellular metabolites, and assemble protein filaments to restrict phage replication. Using cryo-EM, X-ray crystallography, and functional experiments, we connect molecular structures to the defense strategies they enable.
