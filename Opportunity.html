01 / OPPORTUNITIES

Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome enquiries from recent PhD graduates and early-career researchers in structural biology or biochemistry. Positions on externally funded projects will be advertised here as they become available.

You may also explore the ANRF National Postdoctoral Fellowship, DST WISE-KIRAN fellowships, and the IIT Kharagpur Postdoctoral Fellowship. Please contact Dr. Puja Majumder in advance to discuss a suitable project and research proposal. Eligibility and application dates are set by each scheme.
